About

I am currently pursuing a Master of Science in Electrical Engineering at Arizona State University, specializing in Analog and Mixed-Signal IC Design. My work focuses on transistor-level CMOS design, OTA architectures, switched-capacitor systems, and sigma-delta modulation.

I emphasize structured verification methodology including AC, transient, CMRR/PSRR, noise, stability, and PVT analysis using Cadence Virtuoso and Spectre.
